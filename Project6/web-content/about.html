Our Solar System

The Sun holds our solar system together through gravity. Eight planets orbit it, each with its own unique features.

Meet the Planets

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

Venus

A rocky planet with an extremely hot atmosphere.

Earth

Our home planet, with liquid water on its surface and a wide variety of life.

Mars

Known as the Red Planet because of iron-rich dust on its surface.

Jupiter

The largest planet, famous for its Great Red Spot.

Saturn

A gas giant known for its large and beautiful system of rings.

Uranus

An ice giant that rotates on its side compared with most other planets.

Neptune

A distant ice giant known for its powerful winds.

The Search Continues

Scientists use telescopes, satellites, and robotic spacecraft to learn more about planets, stars, and the possibility of life beyond Earth.
